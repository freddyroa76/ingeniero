Interactive visual tool based on GPSA Section 13.1 — Rotate discs or input pressures to estimate BHP/MMSCFD.

How to Use

1

Drag the outer disc to adjust discharge pressure, or the inner disc for suction pressure.

2

You can also type the pressures directly into the inputs below.

3

Results are shown in the center panel: BHP/MMSCFD, compression ratio, and number of stages.

Technical Reference

Standard GPSA Sec. 13.1
Atm. Pressure 14.65 psia
Specific Gravity 0.65
Isent. Exp. (N) 1.26
Suction Temp 80°F
Interstage Temp 120°F
Max CR/Stage 3.8

Formula

BHP/MMSCFD is calculated using the GPSA isentropic equation with a compressor efficiency of 77.5%. The number of stages is automatically determined when CR > 3.8.
